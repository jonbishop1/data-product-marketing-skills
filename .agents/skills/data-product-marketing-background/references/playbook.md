# Marketing background playbook

## Fictional product context

FlowLake is a fictional incremental ingestion product. A synthetic source-to-warehouse sample works, but real data needs approved credentials and reconciliation. Sample completion is not production approval. Update, delete, late-arrival, freshness, and recovery behavior must be verified rather than assumed.

## Decision to resolve

Record source and destination, data sensitivity, update patterns, schema, freshness/correctness requirements, access owner, and workload economics. Define reconciled real-data output and approved recurring operation separately.

## Task-specific method

Capture source systems, data sensitivity, volume and freshness needs, warehouse or runtime, correctness requirements, and who can authorize a representative evaluation.

## Application brief

Deliver: A compact brief with facts, evidence links, open questions, assumptions, the current goal, meaningful success, constraints, and the next decision. Add only material changes to an existing brief.

Do not assume missing capabilities or report proposed tests as completed. A useful answer identifies what is known, finishes the requested artifact within that scope, and names the next bounded verification when needed.

## Review criteria

Do not default to the US, a signup conversion, a funding-based stage, or a fixed channel budget. Product age and funding do not prove product-market fit.

The [shared method examples](shared-methods.md) provide additional templates and cross-product examples; apply only those that fit the product scope.
