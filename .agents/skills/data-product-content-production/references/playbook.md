# Content production playbook

## Fictional product context

FlowLake is a fictional incremental ingestion product. A synthetic source-to-warehouse sample works, but real data needs approved credentials and reconciliation. Sample completion is not production approval. Update, delete, late-arrival, freshness, and recovery behavior must be verified rather than assumed.

## Decision to resolve

For guides state data provenance, shape, transformations, expected invariants, access scope, reconciliation, and operating limits. Label synthetic records and illustrative charts; do not publish a performance conclusion without equivalent, reproducible workloads.

## Task-specific method

Describe dataset shape, provenance and permissions, freshness, transformations, validation, operational limits, and evaluation cost. Avoid publishing private records or cherry-picked performance as a universal result.

## Application brief

Deliver: A finished draft, source and verification notes, any unresolved publication blockers, and an explicit next step appropriate to the reader. Keep internal evidence notes out of public copy unless relevant.

Do not assume missing capabilities or report proposed tests as completed. A useful answer identifies what is known, finishes the requested artifact within that scope, and names the next bounded verification when needed.

## Review criteria

Do not invent customers, quotes, benchmarks, research results, or working code. Respect source licenses and attribution; transform ideas into original task-specific work rather than paraphrasing an entire source.

The [shared method examples](shared-methods.md) provide additional templates and cross-product examples; apply only those that fit the product scope.
