## Product scope

Connectors, pipelines, databases, warehouses, analytics, and other products that move, transform, query, or operationalize data.

## Inputs

Product and supported task, target market, conversion and qualification definitions, landing path, supplied budget ceiling, and available account or CRM data.

## Workflow

1. Define qualified demand and the downstream outcome. Separate clicks, form fills, useful evaluations, recurring value, and paid outcomes.

2. Group queries by task and intent. Exclude proven mismatch; put uncertain commercial, licensing, platform, or support queries in a review group. No hosted service does not rule out licensing or enterprise demand. Undocumented Windows support means unknown fit, not confirmed incompatibility.

3. Verify current platform mechanics using official sources when available; otherwise draft strategy without asserting current feature/policy details. Use supplied budget and observed or explicitly illustrative cost assumptions.

4. Write clean publishable ads from supported behavior and match each to a relevant evaluation page. Keep internal claim-review notes out of headlines and descriptions. Phrases such as "no telemetry claims" are editorial notes, not benefits.

5. Deliver campaign groups, ad drafts, negatives with evidence, uncertain-query review, conversion QA, budget cap if supplied, and review rules. Do not invent minimum spend. Drafting does not authorize account changes or spend.

## Data-product marketing decisions

Tie query groups to supported source/destination workloads and buying context. Qualify access, platform fit, correctness needs, and an evaluation owner; account for delayed enterprise qualification and distinguish students from deployable accounts.

Tie terms to supported workload and connector needs. Qualification may require deployment fit, permissions, data scale, and a credible evaluation owner rather than an inexpensive lead.

## Deliverable

Campaign and ad-group structure, example ads, intent and exclusion rationale, landing-path requirements, budget scenarios, and downstream measurement and decision rules.

## Quality checks

Do not default to business-hours delivery, mobile bid cuts, or a fixed conversion-count rule. Do not claim causality from attributed pipeline or upload sensitive customer data without authorization.
