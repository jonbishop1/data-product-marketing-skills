# Seo audit playbook

## Fictional product context

FlowLake is a fictional incremental ingestion product. A synthetic source-to-warehouse sample works, but real data needs approved credentials and reconciliation. Sample completion is not production approval. Update, delete, late-arrival, freshness, and recovery behavior must be verified rather than assumed.

## Decision to resolve

Inspect connector and workload pages for distinct supported semantics, source evidence, evaluation instructions, and appropriate canonical structure. Check whether thin integration permutations obscure the useful documentation and proof.

## Task-specific method

Inspect connector and destination pages for distinct supported behavior, task evidence, and current limitations. Avoid thin pages generated from every possible source/destination pair.

## Worked application

For this scenario, apply the task-specific method above and deliver: A prioritized issue register with affected URL, observed evidence, severity rationale, proposed fix, owner or dependency, and a reproducible verification step.

Do not assume missing capabilities or report proposed tests as completed. A useful answer identifies what is known, finishes the requested artifact within that scope, and names the next bounded verification when needed.

## Review criteria

Do not claim indexing, traffic loss, rich-result eligibility, or AI citation performance without corresponding evidence. A missing public file is not proof that a whole product is agent-incompatible.

The [shared method examples](shared-methods.md) provide additional templates and cross-product examples; apply only those that fit the product scope.
