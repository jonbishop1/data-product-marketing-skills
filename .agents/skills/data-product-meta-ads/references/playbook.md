# Meta ads playbook

## Fictional product context

FlowLake is a fictional incremental ingestion product. A synthetic source-to-warehouse sample works, but real data needs approved credentials and reconciliation. Sample completion is not production approval. Update, delete, late-arrival, freshness, and recovery behavior must be verified rather than assumed.

## Decision to resolve

Judge whether a synthetic workflow explanation or proof asset reaches a credible evaluator. A content download does not establish platform authority or approved data access. Preserve data privacy and evaluate downstream workload fit.

## Task-specific method

Show a meaningful workflow or evaluation result with its data scope. Avoid real customer records in creative and unsupported “real-time at any scale” promises.

## Application brief

Deliver: Channel-fit judgment, creative briefs and copy, offer and landing path, budget assumptions, qualification definitions, and measurement limitations.

Do not assume missing capabilities or report proposed tests as completed. A useful answer identifies what is known, finishes the requested artifact within that scope, and names the next bounded verification when needed.

## Review criteria

Do not recommend retargeting or customer-list uploads without checking lawful and authorized data use. Cheap clicks or downloads alone do not establish channel fit.
