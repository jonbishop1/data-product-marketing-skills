# Marketing plan playbook

## Fictional product context

FlowLake is a fictional incremental ingestion product. A synthetic source-to-warehouse sample works, but real data needs approved credentials and reconciliation. Sample completion is not production approval. Update, delete, late-arrival, freshness, and recovery behavior must be verified rather than assumed.

## Decision to resolve

Sequence workload-specific demand, access approval, representative correctness evaluation, and operating ownership. If credentials or reconciliation block progress, resolve that path before increasing demos. Measure approved real-data evaluations separately from sample runs.

## Task-specific method

Pair task-specific demand with a credible workload evaluation and a path through access, reconciliation, and platform approval. Separate analyst interest from a deployable account.

## Worked application

For this scenario, apply the task-specific method above and deliver: A decision summary, sequenced plan, owner and resource table, measurement definitions, and an experiment backlog with explicit tradeoffs.

Do not assume missing capabilities or report proposed tests as completed. A useful answer identifies what is known, finishes the requested artifact within that scope, and names the next bounded verification when needed.

## Review criteria

Do not prescribe paid spend, a fixed budget split, or Product Hunt by default. Funding and company age are context, not evidence of readiness to scale.

The [shared method examples](shared-methods.md) provide additional templates and cross-product examples; apply only those that fit the product scope.
